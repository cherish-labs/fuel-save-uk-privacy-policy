FuelSave UK

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

FuelSave UK helps drivers find nearby UK fuel prices. We use location or postcode information only to provide fuel-price search, maps, directions, saved-area features, reminders, and app reliability monitoring.

Information We Collect

FuelSave UK may process the following information when you use the app:

  • Location data: when you choose to use your current location, the app requests your device location to find nearby fuel stations. Location is used while you are using the app.
  • Postcode and search information: when you search by postcode, city, or location, the app uses that information to find nearby fuel stations and may store recent searches on your device.
  • Saved preferences: the app stores your selected fuel type, saved areas, usual station, notification settings, and recent searches on your device so these features work between sessions.
  • Notification settings: if you enable reminders, the app stores your chosen reminder schedule and may include a saved area or usual station in the local notification payload so tapping a reminder can open the relevant search.
  • Diagnostic and crash information: we use Sentry to monitor errors, crashes, logs, and performance. Diagnostic reports may include technical details such as device information, operating system, app version, timestamps, error details, IP address, request headers, and related context needed to investigate problems.
  • Network and connectivity information: the app checks internet connectivity and uses network requests to retrieve postcode, fuel-price, map, and diagnostic information.

FuelSave UK does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, payment details, contacts, photos, camera access, or microphone access.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • show fuel stations and prices near a postcode or current location;
  • display station details, maps, and directions;
  • save areas, recent searches, fuel-type preferences, usual station choices, and reminder settings;
  • send local fuel-price reminders if you enable notifications;
  • check whether the app is online and whether fuel data is available;
  • detect, debug, and improve app reliability, security, and performance.

Location Data

Location access is optional. If you tap the location button or otherwise choose a current-location search, FuelSave UK asks your device for permission and uses your latitude and longitude to search nearby stations. The app may convert your location to a nearby UK postcode to improve search results.

You can also use the app without location permission by entering a postcode or city manually. You can change or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.

Local Storage on Your Device

FuelSave UK stores saved areas, recent searches, chosen fuel type, usual station, reminder preferences, and pending notification information locally on your device using app storage. This helps the app remember your settings and provide reminders. Removing the app or clearing its storage will remove this local information from your device.

Third-Party Services

FuelSave UK uses trusted third-party services to provide core app functionality, including fuel-price data, postcode lookup, maps, directions, cloud hosting, and app reliability monitoring.

These services may process limited technical information, such as IP address, device information, app request metadata, approximate location or postcode search data, and diagnostic information, only as needed to provide their services.

Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only where needed to provide the app, including with service providers listed above, or where required by law, regulation, legal process, security, or fraud-prevention obligations.

Retention

Information saved in the app, such as saved areas and preferences, remains on your device until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Diagnostic information sent to Sentry is retained according to our Sentry project settings and Sentry's retention practices. Fuel-price history in our backend is retained for up to 90 days for trend and data-quality purposes; this data relates to fuel stations and prices, not individual app users.

Your Choices and Rights

You can control your information in several ways:

  • use postcode search instead of device location;
  • deny or revoke location permission in your device settings;
  • turn notifications on or off in the app or in your device settings;
  • remove saved areas or your usual station in the app;
  • clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove local app data from your device;
  • contact us to ask about access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, or portability rights that may apply under UK data protection law.

Children

FuelSave UK is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information processed by the app. No app, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

International Processing

Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers, such as contractual protections offered by those providers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. If changes are material, we may provide additional notice in the app or through the app store listing where appropriate.
